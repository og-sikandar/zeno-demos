Perth pest questions

Are your pest treatments safe for pets and kids?
All of the products used inside and outside your property are free from solvents, gases and aerosols, so you will not smell or breathe in anything. The water based products target insects only, and the rodent treatment targets rats and mice only.
How do I prepare for my pest treatment?
Please leave spider webs and ant mounds alone so the technician can see where your pest problems are. Take any clothes off the washing line and shut your windows. Open up any sheds, garages and areas you would like to be pest free, and clear out any cupboards you need treated.
What should I expect after my pest control treatment?
There will be no smell, and you should see a big drop in pests. Small areas may see more activity for up to two weeks as pests are flushed out. Please leave sprinklers off for two days after your treatment, so ants wander out in search of water.
How often should I have a pest treatment?
Most homes book once or twice a year, depending on location and the pests targeted. It is smart to combine the pests you want treated into one visit.
