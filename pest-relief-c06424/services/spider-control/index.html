Spider Pest Control

At Pest Relief, we provide comprehensive spider control treatment in WA, which involves a thorough inspection, advanced extermination methods, and ongoing control procedures. Whether you have a residential or commercial property, we have the expertise, tools, and techniques to reach every corner of your space and eliminate spiders completely.

Australia is home to a wide range of spider species, and while most of them are harmless or avoid human habitats, some are known for their deadly bites. Therefore, professional spider control is essential if you observe spiders in your surroundings.

Spider Inspection

Our team of local spider extermination specialists in WA are certified and highly experienced. They will conduct a detailed property inspection to identify any spider issues. During this inspection, we will carefully assess and document various factors, including the spider species present, their characteristics, health risks to humans and pets, the extent of the infestation, and more. We will also evaluate and document any possible factors at your property that may have attracted the spider species. After the inspection, you will receive a detailed spider inspection report with our findings, including photographs of the infestations, recommendations, and more.

Spider Treatment Plan

Our Spider Control technicians will design a customized treatment plan for your surroundings after reviewing the spider inspection results.

The plan will consist of treatment procedures, specific instructions for the occupants, service timeline, expected results, and other relevant information. Depending on the inspection, your property may necessitate additional treatment, in which case the follow-up treatment details will also be included in the spider treatment plan.

Spider Extermination

In accordance with the treatment plan, we will conduct spider treatment at your property, utilizing a combination of treatment procedures to provide optimal results for our clients. Our techniques involve fogging, repellents, insecticidal sprays, and powder chemical treatments in ceilings.

At Pest Relief, we exclusively employ approved, family-friendly spider treatment products and procedures that comply with Australian standards. This guarantees a completely safe pest treatment process for our clients, their pets, and the surrounding property environment.

Ongoing Spider Prevention

In cases where your property has conducive conditions for spider infestation, our spider treatment alone may not yield long-term outcomes. Therefore, we will conduct specific repairs and provide techniques and recommendations based on our inspection to decrease the risk of future infestation. Typically, you can manage the spider population on your property by maintaining proper sanitation and upkeep. Reduce clutter and improve ventilation in damp areas of your property. Prune trees and shrubs surrounding your property and maintain your yard to limit safe breeding grounds for pests. Additionally, carry out periodic inspections of your property to identify any property damage, spider entry points, and signs of spider infestation.
