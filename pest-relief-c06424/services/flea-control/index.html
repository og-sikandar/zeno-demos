Flea Pest Control

Do you notice an increasing number of fleas in and around your property? Are you in need of end-of-lease flea treatment and searching for advanced flea pest control for your home or office? Look no further than Pest Relief, the one-stop solution for flea pest control in WA. For years, our experienced and certified exterminators have been known for providing quality flea infestation treatments at a competitive price across WA.

Our specific flea control procedures are designed and applied based on the type of flea and the severity of the infestation in your property. Contact us to experience the difference and make your environment clean, hygienic, and pest-free.

Flea Inspection

As soon as you reach out to us, we initiate our flea eradication service for your home or commercial property. At a time that works best for you, our pest control expert will conduct a thorough inspection of your property. This inspection serves to identify the specific species of fleas that need to be eradicated, examine bites on your pets, analyze the traits and patterns of the flea infestation, and consider other factors. The ultimate goal of this flea control inspection is to ensure a clean and healthy environment for your workplace or home.

Flea Treatment Plan

Based on our findings, we will design and create a flea infestation treatment plan. The flea control treatment plan covers the type of treatment procedure required, instructions for the inhabitants to follow, the expected timeline, and the possible outcome. We will give a detailed explanation of all the steps before we start our flea pest control process. You can ask any questions; we will be happy to clear your doubts.

Flea Extermination

Our approach to flea control involves tailoring our methods to the specific flea species and level of infestation. Depending on the situation, we may provide physical treatment, chemical flea treatment, or a combination of both. Physical treatment involves techniques like vacuuming and fogging, with the addition of heat treatment if necessary.

Alternatively, we also offer a chemical treatment process using insecticides that have been approved by veterinarians. These insecticides are completely safe for both your family and pets and are extremely effective in eradicating fleas. They are able to eliminate adult fleas, as well as their eggs, larvae, and pupae, which are responsible for the infestation.

Ongoing Flea Prevention

While our flea infestation control treatment is incredibly effective, it's important to implement ongoing flea prevention strategies to keep fleas away from your space in the long-term. We will offer personalized recommendations tailored to your specific environment to help you get the most out of these strategies.

Regular cleaning and mopping of your space can significantly reduce the likelihood of a flea infestation. Our customized tips will provide you with additional preventative measures to help ensure that fleas don't return to your space.
