Advanced Termite Treatment Services

Termite infestations can be a nightmare for property owners, often resulting in devastating damage. Have you noticed any signs of termite presence on your property, such as the appearance of earthy, muddy materials in and around your premises?

If you're seeking professional termite treatment to protect your property, look no further than Pest Relief - the foremost pest control experts in Western Australia. Our team of certified and highly skilled pest control technicians will provide you with custom-tailored termite control services. We don't just address the current termite issues at your property, but also take preventative measures and offer ongoing control advice to mitigate the risk of future infestations.

Damage Due To Termite Infestation

  • Termites may not pose a direct threat to human health, but they can certainly have serious financial consequences.
  • These termites are incredibly destructive and can even cause structural damage, resulting in significant financial losses.
  • Termite infestation can also have a negative impact on the cleanliness and upkeep of a property, necessitating more frequent maintenance.

Signs Of Termite Infestation

Subterranean termites leave multiple footprints at the property that help the property confirm the presence of the pest. If you see any of the following termite infestation signs, you can contact us for a termite inspection:

  • Mud shelter tubes of termites around the property, including the foundation
  • Sagging wooden floors and doors frame
  • Deposits of dry mud in the window architraves or door joints
  • Cracked plaster and paint around the wooden structures of the property
  • Easily damaged door jams, skirting boards, or architraves
  • Earthy materials around power points. Power failures without any visible reasons

Termite Inspection

Our pest technicians will conduct a comprehensive inspection of your property to assess the scope of any termite infestation. This inspection will involve identifying and documenting the specific termite species present, as well as determining the level of property damage and any contributing factors that may have led to the infestation.

Upon completing the inspection, we will provide you with a detailed report containing all relevant information, including photographs and recommendations for addressing the infestation.

Termite Treatment Plan

Following the inspection results, our termite treatment specialists will develop a customized treatment plan. This plan will outline the specific extermination procedures, the timeline for treatment, the expected outcome, and any special instructions for the property's inhabitants.

In situations where a follow-up treatment is necessary to fully address the infestation and ensure best termite protection, we will include the relevant details within the treatment plan
