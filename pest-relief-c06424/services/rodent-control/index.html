Importance Of Timely Rodent Treatment

Rodents are a pest group that can make every aspect of healthy living vulnerable and lead to widespread property damage. The following ill effects of rodent infestation highlight the importance of timely pest treatment at your property:

Do you know that rodents multiply in large numbers and excellent survival capabilities? Ignoring the infestation of these pests can sometimes lead to irreversible effects to your health and property.

  • Rodents cause extensive property damage that includes chewing electrical wirings, eating lead and aluminium sheeting, damaging appliances and light fixtures, and more.
  • They are often carriers of various pathogens, and exposure to these pests can risk your health. Rodents spread the plague, salmonellosis, tularemia, leptospirosis, and several other diseases.
  • Rodents contaminate your food by consuming them and leaving their droppings.
  • Chewing and squeezing your fabric and papers, defecating interior, and making creepy noises are the most common hassles rodents make in properties. This forces you to increase the frequency of your maintenance.
  • These pests can mess your property interior and damage your personal properties.
  • Rodents can trigger allergic symptoms and asthma in many people. Studies confirm that exposure to their droppings, saliva, and skin is a major reason for asthma attacks in many households.

Ongoing Rodent Prevention

Long-term results are not guaranteed with a one-time rodents treatment. To ensure lasting effects, we provide ongoing prevention techniques and tactics that are tailored to your property.

In general, the following steps can help reduce pest issues on your property:

  • Regularly remove clutter and keep your property clean and sanitized
  • Store all food products in airtight containers to limit food sources for pests.
  • After feeding your pets, do not leave pet food open and unattended
  • Dispose of food residue in a tightly closed garbage container and empty it regularly.
  • Remove thick vegetation surrounding the property and keep the yard well-maintained.
  • Regularly inspect the property, identify any damage or openings, and repair or close them.
