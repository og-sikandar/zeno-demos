Fly & Maggot Control Treatment Services

If you have started noticing an increasing number of flies hanging around your home or workplace, contact Pest Relief, your local pest control company, for the treatment of flies from your property. We have helped countless businesses and homeowners with our premium quality fly treatment in Perth and its surrounds. We conduct standardized and safe fly infestation treatment procedures to keep your space safe and hygienic. Flies do not attack or bite people or pets, but they carry the potential of causing a health hazard as they transmit many diseases. If the fly infestation is looking critical, contact us as soon as possible for our precise service.

Fly Inspection

Our experienced and skilful exterminator will drive to your property site to conduct a thorough inspection. We conduct an inspection without interrupting your routine work. Our findings will help in verifying the type of fly pest that is causing you trouble and will act accordingly. The inspection will help us to understand the extent of the infestation, damage caused to your property, health and hygiene risks for your family and employees and many other factors.

Fly Treatment Plan

Based on our findings, we will design and create a fly control treatment plan for your property. The fly treatment plan covers the type of treatment procedure required, instructions for the inhabitants to follow, the expected timeline to complete the fly control treatment, and the possible outcome. We will give a detailed explanation of all the steps before we start our fly control treatment process. You can ask any questions; we will be happy to clear your doubts.

Fly Extermination

At Pest Relief, we are committed to providing our customers with a pest-free environment. We generally prefer to use non-insecticide control options rather than chemical treatments. Our certified exterminators use various fly control treatment options based on our findings which include traps, liquid chemical applications, and fly baits. It provides the best possible elimination of the pest fly surrounding your premises.

Ongoing Fly Prevention

One-time fly treatment alone cannot keep flies away for the long-term. There are several tips and tricks that you will have to implement to prevent flies from returning around your property shortly after the eradication treatment. It minimises the likelihood of your property becoming infested again. We will assist you with effective tips and basic fly control tactics to use. Simple tips like regular cleaning of your kitchen, proper maintenance of your yard, proper storage of your food products and having a fly screen installed at windows and doors will be of great help.
