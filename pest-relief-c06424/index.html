Frequently asked questions

What areas do you service? +

We provide professional pest control across greater Perth, including Canning Vale, Southern River, Thornlie, Willetton, Gosnells, Armadale, Baldivis, Rockingham, Mandurah and surrounding suburbs. If you’re unsure whether we cover your area, call us or message through our Google Business Profile.

What pests do you treat? +

We treat ants, cockroaches, spiders (including redbacks), bees and wasps, rodents (rats and mice), termites, bed bugs, fleas, silverfish, flies, and general nuisance pests for both homes and businesses in Perth.

Do you offer same-day service? +

Yes — we offer same-day or next-available appointments in many Perth suburbs for urgent pest issues. Availability depends on current bookings; call our office for immediate assistance.

Do you do commercial pest control? +

Absolutely. We provide commercial pest control Perth-wide for restaurants, cafes, warehouses, offices, retail, strata complexes and builders, with customized integrated pest management plans and documentation for audits.

Do you offer termite inspections? +

Yes. We perform comprehensive termite inspections and provide written reports. We also offer termite barriers, monitoring and treatment options tailored to Perth properties.

Can you remove a bee hive or swarm? +

Yes. We safely remove or relocate bee swarms and hives where possible, using humane and professional methods. For aggressive or inaccessible hives we’ll recommend the safest removal option.

How long does a treatment take? +

Most general pest sprays take 30–90 minutes depending on property size. Specialist jobs (termite work, bed bug treatments, bee removal) vary — we’ll give an estimated duration when we quote.

How soon will I see results after treatment? +

Many pests show reduced activity within 24–72 hours after treatment. For pests with hidden nests or eggs (termites, bed bugs), it can take longer and follow-up visits may be required.
