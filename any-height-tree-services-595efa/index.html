Frequently asked questions

When should I consider removing my tree? +

When it is diseased, damaged or dead, hazardous, or interfering with buildings or structures, needed repairs, or development and construction plans.

Why should I prune my tree? +

To remove dead or weak limbs that pose a hazard, clear electrical wires and buildings, train young trees, stop rubbing branches, gain space and sunlight, keep animals off your roof, and improve shape and curb appeal.

When should you trim your hedge? +

New hedges need formative pruning for their first couple of years, usually in winter or early spring. After that, maintenance trimming is usually once a year for informal hedges and twice a year for formal ones.

Why should I remove my stump? +

A stump left on your property is a tripping hazard, especially for children, it gets in your way, and it may devalue your property.

Do I need a permit to prune my tree? +

As long as you do not remove more than 30% of the canopy, Urban Forestry does not require a permit.

When do I need a permit to remove my tree? +

Under the by-law you need a permit to remove, injure or cut down a tree more than 30 cm (12 inches) across, measured 1.4 m (4.5 feet) above the ground.
