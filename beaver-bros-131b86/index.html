Quick answers

Is the quote free?
Yes. Contact them for your free quote and assessment, for residential and commercial clients.
Which areas do you serve?
Edmonton and area: Spruce Grove, Sherwood Park, Stony Plain, St. Albert, Devon and Beaumont, and as far as Fort Saskatchewan, the Wabamun Lake community and the Alberta Beach community.
What happens to the stump shavings?
Stumps are ground 12 to 20 inches down until every root shows, and the shavings are left on site unless you tell them otherwise.
When can I book Christmas lights?
Bookings start November 1st. The bucket truck is only safe to run down to -20 degrees Celsius, so dates are approximate and booking early in the season is best. The price depends on the linear footage, whether you need lights bought, and whether you want them taken down after the season.
