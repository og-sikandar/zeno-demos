Quick answers

Can you tell me what is wrong over the phone?
Often, yes. If you can describe what the appliance is doing, or the part you need, they can give a repair estimate over the phone.

GE, LG, Samsung, Maytag, Whirlpool, Kenmore, Sub-Zero, Wolf, Viking, Bosch, Thermador, Electrolux, KitchenAid, Jenn-Air and Frigidaire, among others.
Which areas do you cover?
