Common questions

How much does it cost to spray for bugs? +

It depends on the treatment required and species of pests. It also depends on the location of the property. Is the property in Manchester? Or outside of Manchester? To get a quote for any of our pest control treatments please call us on 0161 637 0339.

Does pest control cover mice? +

Yes, Mice are a common pest, in both domestic and commercial premises. They are well adapted to living in close contact with humans and thrive in the conditions that we people provide. They can cause serious damage to property as a result of their constant gnawing activities, and also by damaging, feeding on and contaminating food. Mice carry a number of diseases that can pose a risk to humans and pets. Therefore its necessary to control mice population and include it in every one of our pest control and prevention programs.

Is monthly pest control necessary? +

For food and beverages establishments we recommend regular pest control visits, the frequency of the visits depends on multiple factors. The likelihood of a pest infestation is major factor. Food businesses can be target for various pests such as mice, rats, cockroaches, beetles and ants. Once pests gain access to the property they pose serious health risks by contaminating food.

How often should I pest control my house or business? +

Homes often don’t need regular pest control visits; a one off pest control treatments normally solves the problem completely. However businesses and specially food establishments often need regular pest control visits to prevent and treat any pest activities.

How long does pest control treatment last? +

Pest control treatment visit can last up to an hour in most cases. Specially in the first visit as the pest control technician needs to carry out full pest control survey to assess the problem and find suitable method to clear the pest infestation effectively.

What does pest control include? +

Average pest control treatment would typically include baiting, removal of pest carcasses and proofing. With baiting the pest control technician would place suitable baits for target pests such as rodent baits and cockroach baits. Target pests would then ingest or come in contact with baits and take lethal dose of the insecticide/rodenticide and die. In later visit the pest control technician would remove dead pest carcasses where possible and block access points to insure that the pest problem would not reoccur in the future.

What are pest control services? +

Pest control service controls harmful species of animals, insects or birds. These pest species pose risk to health and also damage crops and property. They cause millions of pounds worth of damage to crops every year therefore it’s very important to find effective pest control methods.
