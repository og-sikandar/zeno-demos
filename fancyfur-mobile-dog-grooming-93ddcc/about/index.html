Two or three dogs a day

FancyFur only grooms two and sometimes 3 dogs a day so that your dog gets groomed in a slower relaxing environment. Extra care and attention is a result of keeping the number of dogs per day to only 2. FancyFur pays attention to detail and thoroughly checks all of your dog's body for any lumps, lesions, or anything that may be of a concern so that you can follow up with your vet if needed.

A full salon in a trailer

FancyFur has been grooming dogs in Tampa since 2013. I drive my truck to your home pulling a 6 X 10 enclosed cargo trailer that has been converted to a full grooming salon for your dogs needs and to get your dog looking his or her best.
