Hi, my name is Michelle

I was born and raised in Tampa, Florida. I'm the owner and groomer for FancyFur, and I graduated from the Tampa Bay grooming academy in November 2013.

I drive my truck to your home pulling a 6 x 10 enclosed cargo trailer that has been converted to a full grooming salon. I only groom two and sometimes three dogs a day, so your dog gets extra care and attention, and I check your dog's body for any lumps or anything that may be a concern so you can follow up with your vet.

Quick answers

What size dogs do you groom?
Toy and small dogs only, up to 25 lbs.
How long does a groom take?
A groom usually takes one and a half to two hours.
What do you need from me?
The trailer hooks up to your outdoor water spigot and electrical outlet, which keeps costs, and prices, lower.
Which shampoo do you use?
Oatmeal shampoo for most dogs; hypo-allergenic fragrance free or flea shampoo when needed, at no extra charge. Free hair bows on request.
