Here comes Benny!

Benny's opened in Palma Ceia, between the Palma Ceia Golf and Country Club and MacDill Air Force Base. Their slogan, "Here Comes Benny!", is known to several generations of Tampa residents.

The original owner, "Benny" Babineau, started his locksmithing career in South Tampa and was a founding member of the Florida West Coast Locksmith Association. Reviewers today name Chris and Adam.
