What is black knot disease? +

Black knot produces rough, black areas that encircle and kill infested parts of a tree. In Alberta it affects the chokecherry family, primarily Mayday and Schubert trees.

When is the best time to remove black knot? +

Black knot is dormant during winter, which is the preferable time to remove it. It is also most visible in the leafless season.

Who does the work? +
