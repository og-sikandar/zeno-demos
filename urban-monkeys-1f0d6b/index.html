Meet Will and the Monkeys

Urban Monkeys Arborists are a small, family run business based in Mosman Park, Perth, Western Australia. Will Goodbourn is the Owner/Operator. He is NPTC qualified and professionally trained in both Australia and internationally.

All work is carried out by Will and their highly qualified, experienced staff, from removing large trees in difficult locations (NT, Australia) with cranes to fine tree pruning, thinning and tree reduction.

As Lead Climber, Will has taken part in annual Climbing Championships. He ranked 15th in Australia in 2012 and held the Northern Territory Climbing Champion title for both 2012 and 2013.

Quick answers

Is the consultation really free?
Yes. One of their qualified arborists will attend your property at a pre-arranged time to assess and discuss your trees. It is completely free with no obligation.
Do you clean palms as well as remove them?
Yes. They climb palms to remove seedpods, old husks, flowers and dead fronds, or remove the entire palm.
Should I tidy up a storm-damaged tree myself?
It is important to call in the professionals after a storm. Careless pruning after a storm can kill the entire limb or lead to more problems later on.
