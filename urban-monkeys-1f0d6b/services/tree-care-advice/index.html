Overview

Trees are considered to be hazardous when they have structural defects and/or partly dead limbs, which are located near a public space. This would be where they are overhanging roads or pavements. Even healthy trees with no obvious signs of decay may be deemed to be hazardous if they interfere with a motorist’s vision, such as at the corner of your property, entrance to driveways etc.

From our many years of experience, we can say that there is no such thing as a totally safe tree, but our trained, professional Arborists can help to advise on what could be done to manage the risks and make the situation safer. Most tree failures are avoidable. A pro-active approach that identifies any potential hazards is always the best course of action.
