Solomon Ostojic, from Montenegro to Ottawa

I'm Solomon Ostojic from the mountains of Montenegro, where people still use wood for cooking and heating all year round. Working with a chainsaw is my everyday job. As a former member of the French Foreign Legion, I spent two years in the Amazonian rainforest with a rifle and a chainsaw as part of my personal equipment, cutting and climbing huge trees on many missions, for helicopter landings or simply for making shelter.

For the last 10 years I have served many customers in the Ottawa region, and I do my best to make them happy. As the owner I personally do all estimates, and I come first and leave last on every job.

I never ask for advance payment, and if you can't pay everything at once, pay when you can; no contracts needed. My goal is to make sure you are satisfied before I leave. I speak English, Français, Português and Srpski.
