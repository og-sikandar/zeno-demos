Wood Chipping Services

With much of our tree waste being turned into nitrogen rich tree mulch, transforming wood chips into mulch for your garden is a smart solution! Trees WA supply premium quality garden mulch to suburbs surrounding the Perth area at affordable prices. Our team are regularly recycling waste that is sourced from the trimming or lopping of bushes, shrubs, and trees. We are never in short supply of wood chips.

Overview

These wood chips can then be recycled and repurposed for garden mulch. This garden mulch is ground down using our high quality machinery and is ideal to keep your garden healthy, moist, and colourful all throughout the year

To find out more about our quality wood chipping services, click on our contact page to get a hold of one of our friendly representatives. Optionally, give us a call on 0449 592 544 to organise an obligation free quote.

MORE ABOUT WOOD CHIPPING

Some pruning can be completed easily at home by yourself but sometimes a professional arborist is required and they can also assist with the green waste. Not everyone has access to a trailer or a ute to transport green waste to the tip, which can also be costly in both time and money. We can visit you and remove all your green waste material or we can leave it behind as mulch so your garden can benefit from its nutritional value.
