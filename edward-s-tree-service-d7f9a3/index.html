Services

Big trees in summer. Snow in winter.

Tree removal and pruning, snow removal, landscaping, equipment rental and disposal, from one crew.

  • Tree removal

    Hazardous, dead or dying trees taken down safely, including near power lines and in confined spaces, and trees in the way of new construction.

  • Tree pruning

    Pruning for residential, commercial and industrial properties, as part of keeping the trees on your land safe and healthy.

  • 24-hour emergency service

    Service 24 hours a day, seven days a week, because accidents can take place at any time.

  • Snow removal

    Residential and commercial snow removal: driveways, parking lots and sidewalks, with plowing, shovelling and salting.

  • Property management and landscaping

    Lawn cutting and maintenance, garden maintenance, fall cleaning and commercial landscaping.

  • Equipment rental

    Chippers, dumpers, a Bobcat, bucket trucks, dump bailers and disposal bins, with help choosing the right equipment.

  • Disposal services

    Disposal bins for rent, waste removal with rubbish disposed of or recycled, and house demolition.

  • Firewood and wood chips

    Firewood sales all year round, and free wood chips: call or stop by.

  • Property repairs and renovations

    As an industrial contractor: property repairs, interior renovations, roofing, irrigation and parking lots.
