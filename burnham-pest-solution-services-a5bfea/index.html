Pests they deal with

Rats and mice are the big one, but there are many more pests B.P.S.S cover, for homes and businesses.

  • Rats and mice

    Rodents in the home or workplace dealt with. It usually takes at least two visits, with the treatment working over two to three weeks.

  • Wasps and bees

    A rapid response to wasp nests in lofts, sheds, walls, air bricks or trees. Bee nests are relocated where possible, with pesticide the last resort.

  • Ants

    Ant nests in the home or workplace treated, including the flying ants of the summer months.

  • Squirrels

    Grey squirrels removed from roofs and lofts before their gnawing reaches the wiring.

  • Cockroaches

    German and Oriental cockroaches treated, with the method chosen for safety in your property.

  • Fleas

    Your property treated for all types of fleas, with advice on treating your cats and dogs too, as both are needed.

  • Proofing

    Removing the pests is only the first stage: they then block the way the pests got in, wherever possible.

  • Commercial contracts

    Shops, kitchens, restaurants, schools and warehouses, with a folder of every visit, treatment and follow-up to show when asked.
