Nine ways to look after your trees.

From palm pruning, their specialty, to power line clearance and emergency storm work.

  • Pruning

    Thinning rather than topping, so the lawn and shrubs underneath get sunlight all year long.

  • Tree removal

    All varieties of trees pruned and removed, with a speciality in large and dangerous trees.

  • Palm pruning and removal

    Palm pruning is their specialty: palms pruned or removed to improve the look of your property.

  • Western Power work

    Power and service line tree clearance carried out in accordance with Western Power regulations.

  • Emergency tree work

    Fallen trees, branches and storm debris removed as quickly as possible, leaving the property safe and accessible.

    Care for the health of your trees, not just lopping, from a qualified arborist.

    Hedges and shrubs trimmed to shape as part of keeping your outdoors at its best.

    Prunings chipped on site, and wood chips provided when available.

Quick answers

How quickly can I get a quote?
They provide an obligation free quotation and advice within 48 hours, and in most cases the job is complete within 5 working days.
Do you take away the prunings?
Yes. All prunings are chipped and worksites cleared of debris. Wood chips can be provided when available.
How deep do you grind stumps?
Stumps or roots are ground to 300 mm below the surface, so ground cover and shrubs can be planted in place of the tree.
Can you clear trees near power lines?
Yes. Power and service line tree clearance is carried out in accordance with Western Power regulations.
What about storm damage?
Fallen trees, branches and debris from storm damage are removed as quickly as possible, leaving your property safe and accessible.
