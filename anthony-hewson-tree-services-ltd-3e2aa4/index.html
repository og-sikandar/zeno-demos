Meet Anthony

My name is Anthony and I am a fully qualified Tree Surgeon based in Hollywood, Birmingham, with over 20 years experience working in the trade.

As a company, myself and my workers pride ourselves on an outstanding, tidy job at fair and competitive prices, paying attention to detail and providing what the customer has asked for.

All goods taken away from your properties are safely disposed of at local allotments and yards, being re-used in various ways.
