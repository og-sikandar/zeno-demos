Who comes out

Pestgon Pest Control is owned and operated by Tushar Pandya. He holds a BSc in Agriculture with a specialisation in entomology and has more than 10 years in pest management.

He is a trained technician for mice, bed bugs, cockroaches, ants, birds and pigeons, and has experience managing and relocating wildlife. He believes in prevention and in showing customers how to keep pests out.
