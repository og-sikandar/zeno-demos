Common questions

Do you offer a 24-hour locksmith service in Perth? +

Yes. 24/7 emergency locksmith assistance for urgent lockouts and related lock problems. Call to confirm attendance arrangements and pricing.

Can you help if I am locked out of my house or office? +

Yes. They assist with residential and commercial lockouts. Tell them the type of door, lock and situation so they can discuss the right service.

Can a locksmith open a door without damaging the lock? +

Often a suitable lock can be opened without significant damage, but the approach depends on the lock’s design and condition. If drilling or replacement is necessary, your locksmith explains the work before going ahead.

Is it better to rekey locks or replace them? +

Rekeying is often worth considering when the lock works properly and you only need old keys to stop working. Replacement may suit better if the lock is damaged, worn or does not meet your security needs.

How do I find a mobile locksmith near me in Perth? +

Call with your Perth suburb and a description of the issue. They can confirm coverage, availability and an estimate based on what you tell them.

How much does an emergency locksmith cost in Perth? +

The price depends on the time of day, the day of the week, the lock type and the work required. The full price list is published, and you can ask for a quote before booking.
