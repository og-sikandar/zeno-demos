Services

Every tree job, one crew.

Lopping, removals, pruning, stumps, palms and land clearing for homes, property managers and businesses.

  • Tree lopping

    Branch removal when limbs have become dangerous through disease, damage or age, or are in the way of something else.

  • Tree removal

    Removing a tree that is old, diseased or damaged enough to threaten falling, or when land is being cleared for another use.

  • Tree pruning and arborist reports

    Pruning that keeps trees strong and balanced, plus arborist assessments and reports to help with council approvals.

  • Stump grinding

    Grinding a left-over stump so completely you will never know a tree grew there.

  • Palm tree removal and cleaning

    Palms removed safely away from buildings and landscaping, or cleaned up so they look better.

  • Land clearing

    Clearing trees and overgrown bush for building or landscaping, keeping the vegetation worth keeping.

Quick answers

What areas do you service? +

Brisbane Treeworx provides tree services to the Brisbane region and surrounding areas, from Logan to Caboolture and everywhere in between. If you are outside this area, please contact us.

How much will it cost to remove a tree? +

Cost depends on the size, location, access and number of trees. It is very difficult to give a price without seeing the tree, which is why we offer a free, no obligation quote and advice to all customers.

Do I need council permission to remove my tree? +

Depending on your suburb and the type of tree, you may need permission from your local council. Always call your council or check their website first. If you are unsure, contact Brisbane Treeworx.

Can I prune my neighbour’s tree if it is overhanging into my yard? +

We recommend you talk to your neighbour first. Brisbane Treeworx can help with lopping a neighbour’s tree and are happy to contact your neighbour on your behalf to discuss the work or arrange access.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We always carry an eftpos machine for debit and credit card payments. We also accept cash, cheque and bank transfer.

Do I need to be at home when you do the work? +

No. As long as we have clear access to the trees, you do not need to be there.

Do you offer pensioner discounts? +

Yes, all pensioners receive 10% off.

Can I keep the mulch? +

Yes. Let us know before the job if you would like to keep the mulch and where you would like it.
