The Serious Risk Posed by Rats

  • Health risks: Rats carry over 35 diseases transmissible to humans, including Hantavirus, Weil’s disease, Listeria, Sa monella, and Toxoplasma gondii.
  • Property damage: They gnaw on electrical wiring, water and gas pipes, insulation, furniture, and walls, potentially causing fires or floods.
  • Rapid reproduction: Rats can breed year-round, with females producing up to 6 litters per year , totaling over 120 offspring , making early intervention crucial.

Aftercare

  • Implement permanent prevention measures: o Store food in rat-proof containers o Keep bins securely closed o Seal entry points around pipes, doors, and vents o Protect animal hutches and runs
  • Monitor the property for signs of new activity
  • Ensure ongoing hygiene and cleanliness

Poison Bait Treatment

  • Baits are placed in secure feeding boxes, trays, or occasionally loose in strategic locations
  • Rats may feed several times before dying; this ensures other rats in the colony are not suspicious
  • Second visit: Technician removes bait and safely disposes of dead rats
  • Duration: Rats may take up to 7 days to feed and succumb

Biocidal Spray

  • Recommended for low-level infestations and loft insulation treatment
  • Applied to affected areas to sanitize and deter remaining activity
  • Price: From £110+VAT

Proofing and Loft Insulation

  • Rat- proofing prevents future access to properties
  • Loft insulation may require removal if contaminated
  • Includes: safe removal, vacuuming, biocide application, and fresh insulation installation
  • Prices depend on loft size – call for a quote

Rat Problems in Drains and Sewers

  • Test baits and camera surveys identify rat breaches in drains
  • One-way sewer valves installed to prevent rats entering buildings
  • Cost-effective alternative to full drain replacement
  • Success depends on property layout and local rat activity

Rodent Problems

Rodents such as Rats, Mice, and Grey Squirrels can not only cause damage to your property, fines, and/or imprisonment for business but they can also spread diseases and viruses to humans.

They can enter your home or business through drains, air bricks, or holes in the building fabric to name but a few. The presence of unwanted fury guests can be stressful. It is important to take immediate action to protect your property and ensure that they never return.
