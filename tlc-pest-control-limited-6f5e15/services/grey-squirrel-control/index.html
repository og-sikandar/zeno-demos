Identifying a Grey Squirrel Infestation

Signs of infestation:

Structural risks: Grey squirrels can damage joists, roof tiles, insulation, electrical wiring, and water storage tanks.

  • Droppings and urine stains
  • Gnaw marks on timber, insulation, or cabling
  • Scratching noises in lofts or walls
  • Nests or “dreys” in lofts or roof spaces
  • Increased squirrel activity in gardens

Facts About Grey Squirrels

  • Origin: North America; introduced to the UK in Victorian times
  • Displacement: Have largely replaced red squirrels in England, Wales, and parts of Scotland
  • Behaviour: Agile, diurnal, highly territorial, strong climbers, and excellent jumpers (6+ m)
  • Communication: Uses tail signals, urine, and glandular secretions for marking territory
  • Nesting: Build dreys (nests) lined with grass, bark, moss, and feathers, often in trees or loft spaces
  • Breeding: Twice a year; gestation ~6 weeks; 3 – 9 young per litter; young leave nest at ~10 weeks
