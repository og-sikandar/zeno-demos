Common questions

Can I still book honeybee removal this year?
Safe honeybee removal is carried out from late March to September. 2027 survey bookings are open now; book before the end of February 2027 to save 10% on the final price, with fixed-cost quotes valid for 6 months.
Can you arrange a loft insulation grant?
No. TLC Pest Control does not provide government grants or apply for funding on behalf of customers. You can still get a free quote for the insulation itself.
Who will come to my property?
All our team members are highly trained and DBS screened. Customers usually meet Locky, who explains what he has found and how it will be resolved.
Can I book without calling?
Yes. You can book an appointment online at any time, send the form, or call 0800 852 7625.
