Sectional Garage Doors

Sectional garage doors provide the greatest flexibility in design choice. A sectional door is made up of horizontal panels which lay parallel with the garage roof when open. We offer a huge array of door options across multiple ranges and colours.

COLORBOND &reg;

State West Garage Door's range of Colorbond® Sectional Garage Doors provides ultimate strength and protection. Our Sectional Doors feature our revolutionary Finger-Safe® design along with a host of quality features. The result is a range of stylish, functional and safe garage doors. Manufactured from genuine BlueScope Steel.

Ultimate Series

Our Ultimate Sectional Garage Doors are all about flaunting absolute street appeal. State West Garage Door's Ultimate Range doors are each distinctive in their own right. You can customise them to include different materials and designs to suit your taste, budget, and preferences.

Timber Look

Achieve the ultimate natural timber look with State West Garage Door's Timba Look Sectional Doors. Add a warm, natural, and timeless look to your home’s facade. We will design your Timba Look Garage Door to suit your budget, style and specifications.

Sunshine Series

The Sunshine Garage Door Series stays true to its name. It allows natural light to shine through your garage door without sacrificing privacy or security. The Sunshine Series features a heavy-duty aluminium frame and translucent sheet panels. You can customise it with an assortment of powder coat frame colours, panel materials and styles. Give your home a complete facelift with a garage door that brightens your garage from the outside in.

Architectural Series

Our Architectural Garage Door Series incorporates state-of-the-art finishes in vogue with clean and modern industrial architecture and design. The Architectural Series features our signature negative-detail look, designed to give you and your architect the ultimate in freedom and choice to create your distinctive look.
