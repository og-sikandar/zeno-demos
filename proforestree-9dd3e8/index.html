Meet Joel McCracken

Proforestree is owned and operated, hands-on, by one of Ontario’s Registered Professional Foresters (RPFs) and arborists, Joel McCracken.

After a Forest Science degree, Joel worked in the forest industry for the provincial government and finally for the City of Toronto before starting Proforestree in 2018.

Joel loves interacting with his many clients. He takes a great deal of pride in his work and loves the reward of seeing a client’s objective met.

Permits, reports and quick answers

Do I need a permit to remove a tree? +

All municipalities have Tree By-Laws that protect trees. We will let you know your rights, and if you require a permit to do any tree work we will prepare the documentation (arborist report and application) required to obtain your permit.

How long does the city take on a permit? +

It takes 2-3 weeks for the City to respond to an application.

Who will look at my trees? +

We use ISA-certified arborists and are owned by one of Ontario’s Registered Professional Foresters (RPFs).

How fast do you respond? +

Questions? Contact us here for a free on site consultation, call or text 416 427 0595 – we respond within an hour.

Which areas do you work in? +

Ninety percent of the work we do is for private homeowners in the Beach, Leslieville, Scarborough, and surrounding areas by referral.

Can I get firewood from you? +

At present we deliver only green wood in sizes you can lift but which have not been split.
