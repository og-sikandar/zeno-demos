Our work

Quick answers

Are you really open at night?
Yes. Google lists Ace Locksmith as open 24 hours, seven days. Call 021 566 406 or 0800 223 562.
Can you make a car key if I have lost all of mine?
Yes. Lost keys are made, cut to code and programmed for almost all vehicles, including transponder, smart and keyless keys.
Which areas do you cover?
Auckland wide. It is a mobile service, so the locksmith comes to your home, workplace or car.
Do you do commercial and access control work?
Yes: commercial locks, restricted master key systems and IQP checks for auto and access control doors (3.1 to 3.3).
