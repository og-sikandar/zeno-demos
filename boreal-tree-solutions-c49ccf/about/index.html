About

Built on a passion of seeing trees thrive, Boreal Tree Solutions is proud to offer exceptional tree care services in Winnipeg and surrounding areas. We take our reputation of excellent customer service seriously. We will always give our best effort, and full attention to every client & job. We firmly believe that educating our clients in tree care is the single greatest service we can offer. We will always work to find the solution that’s right for you and your trees.

The Team

Joel Kosa

Owner ISA Certified Arborist®​​ PR-5381A Qualified Tree Risk Assessor®​​​ Licensed Manitoba Arborist®​

Joel has been living and pursuing all things ‘tree’ since 2013. For nine years he has been refining his knowledge in Arboriculture and cultivating his passion for preserving Winnipeg’s urban forest. If you know Joel, you know he is a tree nerd, always learning about new climbing techniques, plant science and connecting with arborists around the world.

Quinn Monkman

Ground Crew

Quinn has been working for Boreal Tree Solutions for three seasons. You’ll find him assisting with all arboriculture operations from the ground; sending up tools, rigging down wood, keeping the work zone safe, petting clients dogs etc. Quinn’s growing passion for tree care makes him an invaluable part of our team.
