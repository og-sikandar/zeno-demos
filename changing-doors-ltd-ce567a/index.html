Quick answers

Can my garage door be repaired, or does it need replacing?
Often it can be repaired. They supply parts for all makes of garage door regardless of age, and give a free, no obligation site survey and quote either way.
Do you charge for a call out?
No. The 7 days a week call out service has no call out charge, and if needs be they come the same day to diagnose and fix the problem.
Which areas do you cover?
Greater Manchester, Cheshire and the North West, including Stockport, Didsbury, Cheadle, Altrincham, Wilmslow, Macclesfield, Glossop and Warrington.
What types of garage door do you fit?
Up and over, roller, sectional (insulated or non-insulated), side hinged and automated doors, in steel, timber, GRP and UPVC, in standard and bespoke sizes.
Can you automate my existing door?
Yes. Remote control openers can be fitted to new and existing doors, for homes and commercial property.
When are you open?
8am to 7pm Monday to Friday, and 9am to 5pm Saturday and Sunday. Call 0161 429 0005.
