How we work

  1. 1

    Walkthrough and measurements

    We review the fence line, gate locations, access, and what the project really needs.

  2. 2

    Clear scope and recommendation

    If repair makes sense, we say repair. If replacement is the better long-term move, we say that too.

  3. 3

    Build, then a final walkthrough

    Posts, alignment, spacing, hardware, and gate function, then we confirm the result and make sure the project is finished cleanly.

Quick answers

Should I repair or replace my fence? +

Not every fence needs to be replaced. If repair makes sense, they say repair. When repairs keep stacking up, replacement is often the smarter long-term move.

What fence types do you install? +

Wood privacy fences, wrought iron and chain link, with gates for each.

What can you repair? +

Leaning sections, damaged posts, broken boards and gate issues.

How soon will I hear back? +

Once a request is submitted, they call within 1-2 business days to set up an appointment.

Where do you work? +

Katy, Cypress, Richmond, Houston, Sugar Land and Fulshear in Greater Houston, plus Austin and San Antonio in Central Texas.
