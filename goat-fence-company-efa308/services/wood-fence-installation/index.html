Wood Fence Installation in Katy, West Houston & Central Texas

GOAT Fence Company installs wood fences for homeowners who want real backyard privacy, better curb appeal, and a fence line that stays straight. We build cedar and pine wood fences with careful layout, solid posts, and gates that swing and latch the way they should.

Layout Confirmation

Before any digging starts, we verify the fence line, where gates should go, and how the fence transitions around corners, slopes, and existing features. You'll know exactly what's being built and where.

Post Settings

A wood fence is only as strong as its posts. We set posts with alignment and stability in mind so the fence stays straight, holds tension, and stands up to weather and daily use.

Framing + picket installation based on your selected style

We build the fence structure and install pickets to match your chosen style-privacy, board-on-board, shadowbox, or decorative. The goal is clean lines, consistent spacing, and a finished look.

Gate build + hardware installation and alignment

Gates are the most-used part of most fences, so we focus on solid framing, proper bracing, and smooth operation. We install and adjust hardware so gates open and close cleanly and latch correctly.

We keep the jobsite tidy throughout the install and clean up when the work is complete. If removal/haul-off is part of your scope, we'll confirm that up front and handle it accordingly.

Final walkthrough + punch list

We do a final walkthrough with you, confirm gate operation, alignment, and finish details, then address any punch-list items. You'll leave with a fence you feel good about-not a list of &ldquo;we'll come back later.&rdquo;
