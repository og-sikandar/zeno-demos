Common questions

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Toronto? +

In order to remove a tree on your property larger than 30cm in diameter, you'll need to submit an Arborist Report to the city for approval. One of our ISA Certified Arborists will assess the tree and prepare an official document for Urban Forestry to review.

Can you tell me what is wrong with my tree? +

Our arborists can meet with you for a scheduled consultation to assess and diagnose any problems with your tree, and they will also be able to recommend possible treatment plans.

What if something goes wrong on my property? +

New Toronto Tree Care is covered by $2 million in general liability insurance, so you can rest assured that your property is covered.

Do you handle emergencies? +

Contact us for emergency services and we will send out an estimator or a crew as soon as we can.

Is the quote free? +

Get in touch with us today for a free, no obligation quote.

Which areas do you cover? +

Our arborists service Toronto, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Mississauga, North York, and other areas.
