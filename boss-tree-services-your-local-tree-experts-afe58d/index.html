Common questions

Who will work on my trees?
The only people who will work on your trees are Boss Tree Services Professionals. Our team members have many years of experience in the tree industry and are constantly upskilling to keep up to date with new cutting edge techniques. Our team offers a AQF Level 5 Arborist, skilled groundsman, expert climbers and our friendly admin staff.
Are we insured?
YES! We have all necessary insurances for our industry. Extensive Public Liability Insurance for the amount of 20 MILLION to ensure your property is fully covered. Our staff are covered by workover to ensure no liability falls on our customers whilst on their property.
Do I need council approval to conduct work on my trees?
Each Council have different requirements and levels of protected vegetation even if on private property. A quick check with your local council for a vegetation protection check on your property is fast and easy. Just provide your address and your local council will conduct a free check.
