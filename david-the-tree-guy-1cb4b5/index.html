Common questions

What cover do you carry? +

Yes! We carry $5 million in commercial liability insurance and all our crew members are covered by WCB. Safety is our top priority for both our team and your property.

Do you offer emergency tree services 24/7? +

Absolutely. Storm damage doesn't wait for business hours. We have an emergency response team standing by 24/7 to handle hazardous fallen trees, blocked driveways, or storm damage anywhere in the Edmonton area.

When is the best time to prune or trim my trees? +

For most species in Edmonton, structural pruning is best done during the dormant season (late fall through early spring). This reduces stress on the tree and makes it easier to see the structure. However, hazard pruning or deadwood removal can be done safely year-round.

What areas do you serve? +

We serve the entire City of Edmonton and surrounding communities including Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Spruce Grove, Stony Plain, Leduc, and Beaumont.

Do you handle stump grinding after removal? +

Yes, we offer professional stump grinding as an add-on service. We use specialized equipment to grind stumps 6-12 inches below ground level, allowing you to replant grass or new trees in the same spot.
